# Manufacturing Digitalization with MES

## Overview
Leading the transition from paper-based tracking to a fully digital Manufacturing Execution System (MES).

### Key Features
- **Real-time Visibility**: Live dashboards on the shop floor showing OEE and output.
- **Downtime Reason Codes**: Operators categorize stops via touchscreens for accurate data.
- **Paperless Workflow**: Eliminated manual logbooks and transcription errors.

## ROI
The improved visibility and faster reaction times to downtime events resulted in **$150,000 USD** in annual operational savings.
