# LPG Gas Consumption

## Overview
Energy efficiency is a key KPI for sustainable manufacturing. This dashboard tracks LPG consumption per metric ton of production.

### Key Features
- **Efficiency Metrics**: Calculates m³ of gas per ton of product.
- **Oven Performance**: Compares efficiency across different ovens.
- **Production Correlation**: Correlates high consumption with specific product types or operational conditions.

## Impact
This project was crucial for achieving ISO 50001 Energy Management certification and identified inefficiencies that saved **$50,000 USD** in annual energy costs.
